{# ── Features ── #}

Everything built around the album

No distractions. No algorithms forcing singles on you. Just albums, your opinions, and a community that gets it.

Build your collections

Organise albums into collections that make sense to you. Keep them private or share them publicly.

Like and dislike

A simple, honest rating system. No five-star overthinking. Like it or dislike it — your collection reflects your actual taste.

Tag the mood

Apply mood tags to albums — Hype, Melancholic, Focused, Dreamy. See what moods other listeners are feeling from the same record.

Discuss, vote, reply

Comment on albums with threaded replies and Reddit-style voting. Real conversations about real music.

Full tracklists

Every album detail page shows the complete tracklist with track lengths and total album duration, pulled from Spotify.

Artist pages

Click any artist name to see their full discography, with your ratings and community mood data inline.
